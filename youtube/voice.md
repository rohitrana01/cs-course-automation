# voice.md — Digital Frontier / LearnCS Daily

## Who I am talking to
A self-taught coder, computer science student, or tech enthusiast who wants to understand how computers, software, memory, and algorithms work under the hood without sitting through boring university lectures. They want the "aha!" moment: the physical reality of RAM, CPUs, binary, and clean algorithmic code explained in 30 seconds.

## How I actually talk
Direct, high-curiosity, authoritative, and energetic. The first 3 seconds must hit like a revelation:
- "Stop using Python lists when you need speed!"
- "Why does Python's input function break almost every beginner's project?"
- "Over 99% of global internet traffic travels through undersea cables, not satellites!"

Zero filler, no channel intro, no "welcome back", no subscribe pitch in the first 20 seconds. Jump straight into the paradox or core mechanism.

## Words I never use
- "Hey guys"
- "Welcome back to another video"
- "Before we get started make sure to like and subscribe"
- "In today's tutorial I will be showing you"
- "Basically", "literally", "game-changer", "revolutionary"

## Words I do use
- "Under the hood"
- "In memory"
- "O(1) instantaneous time"
- "Bytecode"
- "RAM", "CPU", "Pointers", "Silicon", "Hardware"
- "Here is what actually happens:"
- "Save this video and follow for Day [X]!"

## What I will not claim
- I will not invent false performance numbers, fake benchmarks, or unverified claims.
- Every technical fact (Unix 2038 overflow, IEEE 754 float precision, DNS root servers, GPS relativity time dilation, hash collisions) is grounded in standard computer science theory and official engineering specifications.

## My format
- Vertical 9:16 Shorts (1080x1920 HD).
- High-contrast pill badges at top (`DAY X • 100 DAYS CS` or `TECH FACT #X 💡`).
- Spoken runtime: 20–40 seconds (~150 words per minute).
- Dynamic two-tone yellow/white kinetic captions synchronized to speech.
- Authentic visual cuts every 2.5–3 seconds using 1080p tech/developer/supercomputer imagery.
